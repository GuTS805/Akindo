How it works

How it works

  1. Deposit BTC. You get mbLP shares priced from the Mezo oracle, so a moved pool cannot dilute you.
  2. Borrow. Most BTC becomes collateral in one MUSD trove at ~300% CR; the vault borrows MUSD against it.
  3. Provide liquidity. MUSD plus the rest of the BTC go into the BTC/MUSD pool; the LP is staked in the gauge.
  4. Stay balanced without swaps. Debt is held at NAV / (target CR + 1) by borrowing, repaying and moving BTC between trove and pool.
  5. Exit any time. Withdraw your slice. If an exit would leave less than MUSD's minimum debt, the vault unwinds instead of blocking you.

Why Mezo needs this live from Mezo mainnet

BTC locked in veBTC – earning governance, not liquidity
BTC in BTC/MUSD pools – volatile + concentrated pool
Gauge rewards for that liquidity – MEZO per day
MUSD supply – every deposit adds MUSD debt

The gauges already pay for BTC/MUSD liquidity. What is missing is a way to provide it from plain BTC without running a trove by hand. mbLP is that way.

Built for a thin market

Rebalance never swaps

Borrows, repays and moves BTC between trove and pool to hold the target ratio. It never trades against the pool.

Oracle-guarded

Every pool action checks the pool price against Mezo's oracle and waits if it drifts outside the band.

Manipulation-resistant shares

LP is valued at 2·√(k·price) from the oracle, so trading the pool cannot inflate or dilute shares.

Exits leave the pool alone

A leaver's MUSD gap settles inside the vault at the oracle price + 0.5%, so exits do not walk the thin pool.

Nobody gets stuck

An exit that would break MUSD's minimum debt unwinds the position instead of blocking a large holder.

No owner

Fixed parameters, no upgrades, verified source. Rebalance and harvest are open to anyone.
