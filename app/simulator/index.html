How this is calculated

  • The vault starts at its target: collateral worth 3× the debt, and LP holding the debt's worth of MUSD plus the same worth of BTC. So 75% of the BTC is collateral and 25% is in the pool.
  • After a price move by factor r, collateral is worth 3D·r, the LP 2D·√r (constant-product pool) and the debt D plus interest.
  • Rewards: LP APR × LP value × (1 − 10% fee) × time, scaled by the MEZO price change. Staked LPs get no swap fees.
  • Rebalancing is not modelled. In practice the keeper borrows or repays when the ratio leaves 250–350%, which changes the path but not the overall shape.
  • An estimate for decisions, not a promise: APR moves with MEZO emissions, MEZO's price and how much liquidity joins the gauge.
