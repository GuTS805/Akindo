Why a first deposit is easy

  • One asset. You only need BTC. No MUSD, no veBTC lock, no counterparty waiting for your offer.
  • One transaction. Trove, borrow, pool deposit and gauge stake happen together.
  • Always exitable. Withdraw any share at any time, including in MUSD's Recovery Mode.
  • Nothing to manage. A keeper and anyone's call to rebalance() keep the ratio in its band.

Tell us what broke

Try something odd: a tiny deposit, a deposit and an immediate full withdrawal, a withdrawal in several parts. If anything surprises you, the quickest route is an issue on GitHub. Attack ideas are welcome on Try to break it.
