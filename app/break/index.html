Live attacks v6

Economic attacks, measured in tests that run on every push

A contract can refuse every bad call and still be milked by well-timed trades. These tests give an attacker their own capital, let them push the pool to the edge of the oracle band around someone else's transaction, and push it back. The number is the attacker's profit in sats (1 BTC = 100,000,000), the least negative of pushing the pool up or down. Negative means the attacker paid to try.

Reproduce: cd contracts && forge test --match-contract SandwichTest -vv. An earlier version of the vault lost here: a sandwich around a keeper step earned about 0.002 BTC per 20 BTC deployed at a 6% band, which is why one deployment step now adds at most a tenth of the pool.

    What we do not claim

    • The code is unaudited. This page is evidence, not an audit.
    • The vault trusts Mezo's price feed. A stale oracle pauses deposits and trove exits until it recovers.
    • A thin pool can delay the last exit, and in Recovery Mode only about a quarter of the NAV beyond idle assets can leave at once.
    • Test BTC and MUSD have no value. Nothing here is investment advice.
